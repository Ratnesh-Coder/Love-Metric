    Relationship patterns

    Recognizable communication patterns detected in this chat, each backed by measurable evidence. These describe communication behavior, not psychological facts.

    What the data cannot tell you

    This analysis looks at communication behavior only. Chat data alone cannot reliably determine:
    • Whether someone is secretly in love
    • Whether someone is faithful, or cheating
    • Whether a relationship will last
    • Whether someone is attracted to another person
    • Anyone's psychological diagnosis
    • Someone's true feelings outside the conversation
